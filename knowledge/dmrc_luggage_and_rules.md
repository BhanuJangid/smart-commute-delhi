# Delhi Metro luggage and prohibited items

Last checked: November 2026. Rules are enforced by security staff at the station, and limits have been revised
before, so confirm with DMRC or the station security desk.

## Luggage size and weight

There are two figures in circulation, and they come from different dates:

- DMRC's long-standing passenger guidance says to limit baggage to 15 kg and a size of 60 cm length, 45 cm
  breadth and 25 cm height. In 2017 steel frames were placed at baggage scanners at some stations to enforce the
  size.
- In August 2019 the central government amended the Metro Railways (Carriage and Ticket) Rules. The amended
  rule allows one bag of personal belongings up to 80 cm x 50 cm x 30 cm and 25 kg gross weight. For dedicated
  airport metro trains one bag of up to 32 kg is allowed. Bundles are not permitted, and larger baggage needs
  prior approval from the metro administration.

If you travel with large luggage, the safe approach is to keep to the smaller limit or ask staff at the entry
gate.

## Prohibited items

Items that are not allowed include:

- explosives, firearms and ammunition
- knives, daggers, swords, cleavers, screwdrivers, wrenches, pliers and other sharp or cutting tools
- flammable solids and liquids, petroleum products, acids and other corrosive chemicals
- wet batteries and radioactive substances
- animals and pets

## Food, cigarettes and alcohol

- Food may be carried, but eating inside metro premises and coaches is not allowed.
- One packet of cigarettes and one lighter or matchbox per passenger is reported to be allowed.
- Drinking alcohol inside metro premises is strictly prohibited.

## Security checks

Bags go through X-ray scanners and security staff can inspect suspicious items by hand. Prohibited items can be
confiscated and entry can be denied.
