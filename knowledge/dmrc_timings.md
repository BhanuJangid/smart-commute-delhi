# Delhi Metro timings and frequency

Last checked: November 2026. Timings differ by line, direction and station and can change on holidays or during
special events. For an exact first or last train, check the DMRC app or the station display.

## General operating window

Most services start between roughly 5:00 AM and 6:00 AM and the last trains leave between roughly 10:00 PM and
11:30 PM, depending on the line and the station.

## Examples reported by line

- The Airport Express Line is reported to run from about 4:45 AM to about 11:30 PM.
- On the Magenta Line, the first train from Janakpuri West is reported around 5:10 AM.
- On the Yellow Line, first trains from the northern terminals are reported between about 5:00 AM and 6:00 AM.
- On the Blue Line, first trains are reported around 6:00 AM from the Dwarka Sector 21 and Vaishali ends, with
  last trains around 10:50 PM to 11:05 PM.

These are examples from third-party timetable pages, not an official schedule.

## Frequency

Trains run every few minutes at busy times (roughly 2 to 5 minutes) and less often off-peak (roughly 5 to 10
minutes). The Airport Express Line runs about every 10 minutes.

## Sources

Aggregated third-party timetable and fare information pages (Square Yards, MetroEasy, AgateTravel). These are
indicative only.
