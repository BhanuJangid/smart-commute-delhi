# Delhi Metro fares, smart cards and discounts

Last checked: November 2026. Always confirm on the official DMRC website or app, because fares and offers change.

## Current fare slabs (effective 25 August 2025)

DMRC revised its fares on 25 August 2025, the first increase since 2017. Fares depend on the distance travelled:

| Distance | Normal-day fare |
|----------|-----------------|
| 0 to 2 km | Rs 11 |
| 2 to 5 km | Rs 21 |
| 5 to 12 km | Rs 32 |
| 12 to 21 km | Rs 43 |
| 21 to 32 km | Rs 54 |
| More than 32 km | Rs 64 |

The increase was between Rs 1 and Rs 4 on most lines, and up to Rs 5 on the Airport Express Line. Before this
revision the fares ranged from Rs 10 to Rs 60.

## Sundays and national holidays

Cheaper slabs apply on Sundays and national holidays. Reported examples: journeys of more than 32 km cost Rs 54
instead of Rs 64, the 12 to 21 km slab costs Rs 32, and journeys up to 5 km are capped at Rs 11.

## Airport Express Line

The Airport Express Line has its own fare table, separate from the main network slabs.

## Smart card and NCMC discounts

- Smart card users get a 10 percent discount on every journey.
- An extra 10 percent applies during off-peak hours, so the total can reach 20 percent.
- Off-peak hours are: from the start of service until 8 AM, from 12 noon to 5 PM, and from 9 PM until the end of service.
- NCMC (National Common Mobility Card) holders get the same smart card benefits.
- Multiple Journey QR tickets (MJQRT) are reported to get a flat 20 percent discount during off-peak hours.
- Sources differ on whether the off-peak discount depends on the entry time or the exit time at the station, so
  check the current DMRC rule before relying on either.

## Women's coach

The first coach of every train is reserved for women.
